Frontlines

Two kingdoms, one river. You never command a soldier. You draw the roads they march on, and the armies sort out the rest.

  • Drag from a blue town to draw a march route. Tap a town to choose its troops or clear its route.
  • End a route on an enemy or grey town to attack it, or on open ground to gather an army. Drag from a gathered army to send it on.
  • Big groups hit harder, troops arriving at a run hit hardest, and everyone fights better near home.
  • Farms and gold mines earn gold. Spend it in the tray on forts, watchtowers, bridges, barracks and more farms. A mine works for whoever holds the land around it. The enemy farms, mines and builds too.
  • Terrain matters. Roads are fast: troops march 40% quicker on them. Archers on hills shoot further, and attacking uphill is weaker. Forests slow troops but shelter them from arrows. Fords let anyone wade across the river, slowly.
  • The enemy gathers at the bridges and sometimes fakes an attack. Take Redmarsh to win; lose Ashford and the war is over.

    How Frontlines works

    Rules at a glance

    Orders

    • Drag from a town to draw its march route. New soldiers follow it. The line snaps to roads; hold Shift to draw freehand.
    • End on a town to attack it, or on open ground to gather an army there.
    • Drag from a gathered army to send it on. Free, costs no ink.
    • Tap a town to pick its troops or clear its route.
    • Charge (every 25s): troops in the circle sprint and hit harder.
    • Space pauses, Esc cancels. Hold Shift and hover to inspect anything.
    • Scroll or pinch to zoom in on the fighting, drag open ground to look around, double-click to zoom in or back out.

    Troops

    • Spearmen ▲ tough, stop cavalry charges. Beat cavalry.
    • Archers ● shoot from range, fragile up close. Beat spearmen.
    • Cavalry ■ fast, triple charge damage, train slowly. Beat archers.

    Combat

    • Each nearby ally adds 6% damage, up to +60%.
    • Troops fight 40% tougher near their own towns, 55% inside a fort's circle.
    • A town falls when enough enemies march into it to empty its defence ring.

    Terrain

    • Roads: march 40% faster.
    • Woods: 35% slower, arrows do half damage.
    • Hills: archers +10 range per contour; downhill hits +25%, uphill −20%.
    • Fords: anyone can wade across at 45% speed.

    Economy

    • Farms earn about 7 gold a week for whoever holds their town.
    • Gold mines earn 27 a week for whoever holds the land around them.
    • Buy in the tray: farm 30, watchtower 40, fort 60, barracks 70, bridge 80. Each repeat costs 50% more.
    • Route ink: 600 plus 300 per town you hold.
    • Each week: one free reinforcement, and the enemy grows stronger.
